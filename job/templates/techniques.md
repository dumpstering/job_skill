# Techniques

The lab techniques, instruments and methods you have used, with the words
postings use for them. They drive the Techniques section of your resume,
the fit check, keyword coverage, and "how many years of X" answers. The /job
skill sets this up with you once and adds to it only after you confirm. It
copies the file to backups\ before every change.

One technique per line (techniques.md is read only from lines that start
with "- " and have a level):

    - <technique> | aliases: <other name>; <other name> | level: hands-on | years_ok: yes | evidence: L-0012, L-0031

- aliases: other names for exactly the same thing (qPCR for real-time PCR),
  never a related technique.
- level: hands-on (you ran it yourself in a job, project or completed
  course) or familiar. Only hands-on techniques go on a resume as a skill.
- years_ok: yes lets a form's "how many years of X" question be answered from
  the dated roles of the evidence entries; no means you answer it yourself.
- evidence: the achievement library IDs that show you using it.
