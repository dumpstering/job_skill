# BambooHR

Links on `<company>.bamboohr.com/careers/<id>` (older links:
`/jobs/view.php?id=`). No account.

**How the runner reads it.** `<company>.bamboohr.com/careers/list` (every
opening, JSON) and `/careers/<id>/detail` (description, `datePosted`,
compensation). An unknown or closed company page redirects to
www.bamboohr.com; that counts as gone only when it happens twice.

**The form.**
- One page: contact, resume, cover letter, a few questions. Attach first.
- A CAPTCHA shows up most of the time on submit. It is the user's part; the
  handover says so, and the `s` reply comes after they solve it and click
  Submit.
- Date and dropdown fields are plain selects: pick the option by its text.
