# JazzHR

Links on `<company>.applytojob.com/apply/<id>/...`. No account.

**How the runner reads it.** No JSON list: the server-rendered list at
`<company>.applytojob.com/apply/jobs` links to each job, and every detail
page carries schema.org JobPosting data with `datePosted`. A company that
left JazzHR redirects to jazzhr.com (gone after twice).

**The form.**
- One page; attach first. The detail page loads reCAPTCHA, and a CAPTCHA
  shows up on most submits: the user's part.
- Some forms ask for a typed signature or an "I certify" box at the end:
  always the user's.
